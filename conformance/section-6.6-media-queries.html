Section 6.6: Media Queries Conformance Test

Exercises all permitted media query features: min-width, max-width, exact width, prefers-color-scheme (light and dark), and combining, comma alternative querying, and print media.

1. Dimension Constraints (min-width, max-width, width)

min-width: @media (min-width: 600px)

Condition: (min-width: 600px)

max-width: @media (max-width: 799px)

Condition: (max-width: 799px)

exact width: @media (width: 800px)

Condition: (width: 800px)

2. Combinators (and) & Alternatives (comma)

and: @media (min-width: 500px) and (max-width: 900px)

Condition: (min-width: 500px) and (max-width: 900px)

comma (OR): @media (max-width: 450px), (min-width: 750px)

Condition: (max-width: 450px), (min-width: 750px)

3. Color Schemes (prefers-color-scheme: dark | light)

prefers-color-scheme: dark

Condition: (prefers-color-scheme: dark)

prefers-color-scheme: light

Condition: (prefers-color-scheme: light)

4. Print Media (@media print)

@media print

Condition: @media print
